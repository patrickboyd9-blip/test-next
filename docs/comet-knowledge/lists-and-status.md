# Lists and statuses

You pick who gets the mailer after the proof. Saved lists live in the address book on your account. Add one person, or upload a spreadsheet and match the columns. A list needs at least one complete address. The same list can be used on the next mailer.

Statuses you may see: Designing, Proof ready, Printing, In the mail, Delivered. A proof that is ready is waiting for you. Delivered on a sample screen is not live tracking, and it is not a reply count.

A follow-up needs a list of people you are allowed to mail. This screen does not invent who replied.
