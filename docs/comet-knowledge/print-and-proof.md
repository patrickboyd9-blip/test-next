# Postcard size and the address side

A 5 by 8 postcard finishes at 8 inches wide and 5 inches tall. A 6 by 9 finishes at 9 inches wide and 6 inches tall. Each side keeps a quarter inch of bleed past the trim.

The address side keeps two blank areas, measured from the bottom-right of the trim. On a 5 by 8, the address area is about 3.7 inches wide and 2.9 inches tall. A blank strip for the barcode is about 6 inches wide and 0.6 inches tall. On a 6 by 9, the address area is about 3.7 inches wide and 3.0 inches tall, and the barcode strip is about 6 inches wide and 0.7 inches tall. Do not put type, photos, logos, or offer bars in those areas. The barcode strip stays plain white.

Nothing prints until you approve the proof. The proof is a preview until you do.
