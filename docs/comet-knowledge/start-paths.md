# How to start a mailer

Modern Mail offers four ways to start.

Chat with Comet. Tell Comet your goal. Comet asks what you want people to do, then who should get it, then proposes the postcard.

Upload your own design. Add a front and a back. They are fitted to the postcard. You still see a proof before anything prints.

Start from a template. Pick a mailer for your kind of business, then add your name, phone, and offer.

Have our team design it. Share your logo, offer, and style. A person builds the postcard. That path ends when the request is noted. It does not mail anything.

After a design path, the steps are Proof, then who it goes to, then the full price and Approve & mail.
